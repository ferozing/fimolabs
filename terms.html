The service

Sona, a voice assistant, calls your parent once a day on their normal phone at a time you choose. After each call, you get a short summary on WhatsApp covering how they are, their medicines, mood, food and sleep, and something to ask them. If your parent does not answer, we try again and let you know. Summaries are generated by AI from the call and may sometimes be incomplete or wrong, so please use them as a prompt to call, not as a record you rely on.

You must have your parent's consent before you add them. You confirm this before the first call. You are responsible for keeping their details accurate and for pausing or stopping the calls if they no longer want them.
